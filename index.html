"What are our locations?"

OBMC aims to educated children around the philippines, we are teaching these children in: Greenhills San Juan City, Fairview Quezon City, Las Piñas B.F Homes, Sta.Ana Manila City, and Angeles City Pampanga.

"What are our programs?"

  • Casa Programs: Here, we help children ages 3-5 to develop and learn within their enviornment. We help them develop their intelligence, senses, and their own character by using the items provided by Montessori.
  • Primary Programs: From Grades 1-3 we teach your child more complex ideals and lessons. Here we bring them to a prepare enviornment and help them grow to gain independence and confidence in the word, from ages 6-8.
  • Intermediate Programs: From grades 4-6, we aim to teach your children abour responsibility and care for the enviornment. And we aim to enhance their academic capablities, social skills, problem solving and leadership skills.
  • Junior High Programs: From Grades 7-10, we focus on your kids psychological and social development. We prepare them and get them ready for adult responsibilities, and strengthen their critical skills and collaboration skills, and their independence.
  • Senior High Programs: Finally, from grades 11-12, we provide students with the knowledge and neccessities needed for future succes. With a GAS plus curriculum, we give students the flexibility in exploring career choices.
